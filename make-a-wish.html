An ancient belief

Your wish is a contract
with the universe

Across cultures — from the sky lantern festivals of Thailand and Taiwan to the floating diyas of Diwali — lighting a flame and releasing it upward has always been an act of surrender and intention. The flame is your desire. The act of letting go signals to the universe that you are ready to receive. But ancient wisdom adds one condition: a wish written consciously is not a passive prayer. It is a declaration of willingness. You are not asking for something to happen to you. You are announcing that you are ready to make it happen — and inviting the universe to meet you halfway.
